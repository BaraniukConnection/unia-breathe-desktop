A desktop with room to breathe.

A calm, glassy shell for Hyprland. Glass bubbles instead of a panel, a tile menu, a control centre, settings in their own space, and windows you pick up by a drop in their border.

Design

Made to stay out of the way

Unia Breathe Desktop is the Hyprland desktop of the Unia family: deep emerald glass, one accent, and nothing on screen that doesn't earn its place.

air

Room to breathe

No solid panel. A few glass bubbles on top with the wallpaper between them, and a dock that stays hidden until you reach for it.

location_on

Where you expect it

The clock in the middle, status and power on the right, desks on the left. Super+I for settings, Super+R for apps, Print for screenshots.

pan_tool

Windows you can hold

Every window has a drop in its top border. Drag it, and the neighbours make room before you let go.

palette

One accent, yours

Glass, text and ground are tinted from the accent you pick. Turn blur off and every surface goes solid.

The top bar

Bubbles, not a panel

Each group floats on its own. Hover the status for details, scroll over volume to change it, and click the bell for the control centre.

  • wifiWi-Fi and Bluetooth tiles that open into networks (with a password field) and devices
  • push_pinSix slots for the settings you open most
  • notificationsOur own notification server with history, actions and Do Not Disturb
  • eventA month calendar with your next Google Calendar event, read-only

TileMenu

Your apps, in folders of tiles

The dock waits at the bottom edge with five favourites. The arrow opens a panel of folders you arrange yourself.

  • grid_viewFour tile sizes: small, medium, wide and large
  • scheduleLive tiles show the time, the next event or what is playing
  • drag_panDrag tiles and whole folders; drop between folders to start a new one
  • searchStart typing to search, or switch to All Apps

WindowDrop

Pick a window up by its drop

A small notch grows out of the top border as the cursor comes close. Drag it, or hold Super and drag anywhere, and the window shrinks to a square while a translucent placeholder shows where it will land.

  • touch_appClick the drop for Minimize, Fullscreen, Float and Close
  • aspect_ratioSize: a dashed cross splits the window into quarters; drag from one to resize those two sides
  • arrow_forwardDesk: click for the next desk, hover for the list

SettingsBox

Settings in their own space

Super+I slides a special workspace over your desk: categories on the left, the right app on the right, laid out as one window. The apps you already know open in place.

  • format_paintTheme: a main accent and four secondary colours, from a palette or as hex
  • blur_onWindow opacity, and blur you can turn off for solid surfaces
  • keyboardBuilt-in pages for date and time, layouts, borders and the mouse, all usable from the keyboard

Scenarios

Save a desk, open it again

A scenario remembers which apps were on a desk and where. Open it and the apps launch and fall back into place.

  • dashboardCards with a thumbnail of each layout
  • desktop_windowsOpen desks for every monitor, with + for a new one

Screenshots

Capture, in your way

Print freezes the screen and offers an area, a screen or a window, as a picture or a video, with or without the pointer. Shift+Print and Alt+Print skip the picker.

  • content_pasteCopied to the clipboard and saved to Pictures/Screenshots
  • videocamRecordings go to Videos/Screencasts, with a red dot on the bar while they run

Install

You need Hyprland 0.56 or newer, Quickshell 0.2.1 or newer, and the Manrope and Material Symbols Outlined fonts. The installer only creates symlinks and is safe to run again.

# get it
git clone https://github.com/BaraniukConnection/unia-breathe-desktop ~/unia-breathe
cd ~/unia-breathe

# link the shell, scripts, lock screen and palettes;
# source hypr/unia.conf from hyprland.conf
./install.sh

# then pick key bindings from
# hypr/binds.example.conf and log in again

Under the hood

Quickshell draws everything in QML. Hyprland blurs every unia-* surface, applies settings live through hyprctl keyword, and lays out WindowDrop's placeholder, so the preview matches where a window really lands.
